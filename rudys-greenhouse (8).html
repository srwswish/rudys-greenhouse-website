Tips and step-by-step guides to help your plants thrive in Utah's unique climate. From soil prep to harvest, we've got you covered.

Spring · Annuals
🌸

Planting Annual Flowers

  • 1Wait until after last frost (mid-May in Cache Valley).
  • 2Amend soil with compost — 2–3 inches worked in 6" deep.
  • 3Space plants 8–12" apart for good airflow and growth.
  • 4Water deeply at the base; avoid wetting foliage.
  • 5Deadhead spent blooms weekly to encourage reblooming.

💡 Petunias and marigolds thrive in Utah's sunny summers with 6+ hours of direct light.

Spring / Fall · Perennials
🌿

Establishing Perennials

  • 1Plant in early spring or fall when temperatures are mild.
  • 2Dig a hole twice as wide as the root ball, same depth.
  • 3Backfill with native soil mixed with compost (50/50).
  • 4Water thoroughly for the first season — weekly if no rain.
  • 5Mulch 2–3" around the base to retain moisture in summer.

💡 Hostas and coneflowers are top performers in Northern Utah landscapes.

Spring · Vegetables
🥕

Starting a Vegetable Garden

  • 1Choose a spot with 8+ hours of sun per day.
  • 2Build or prep raised beds — 12" deep soil is ideal.
  • 3Set tomatoes and peppers out after May 15 in Logan.
  • 4Feed plants every 2 weeks with balanced vegetable fertilizer.
  • 5Use cages or stakes early — before plants need them.

💡 Raised beds warm up faster in spring — a 2–3 week head start on the season!

Fall · Seasonal Plants
🍂

Fall Seasonal Planting

  • 1Plant mums and kale in late August–September.
  • 2Choose containers with drainage holes and fresh potting mix.
  • 3Mix textures: tall grasses, mums, ornamental kale, gourds.
  • 4Water consistently; don't let pots sit in standing water.
  • 5Bring tender plants in before first hard freeze.

💡 Ornamental kale gets more vibrant color after a light frost — don't rush it inside!

Year-Round · Watering
💧

Watering & Soil Basics

  • 1Water deeply and infrequently — 1" per week is the goal.
  • 2Check moisture 2" below surface before watering again.
  • 3Utah's alkaline soil may need sulfur for acid-loving plants.
  • 4Add organic compost each spring to improve any soil type.
  • 5Drip irrigation saves up to 50% more water than overhead sprinklers.

💡 Early morning watering reduces evaporation and prevents fungal issues overnight.

Spring · Containers
🪴

Container Garden Success

  • 1Use quality potting mix — never garden soil in containers.
  • 2Follow "Thriller, Filler, Spiller" for beautiful pots.
  • 3Containers dry out fast — check daily in summer heat.
  • 4Feed with slow-release fertilizer at planting, liquid bi-weekly.
  • 5Deadhead and trim regularly to keep containers full and tidy.

💡 Dark containers absorb more heat — great in spring, but can stress roots in peak summer.
